Overview

From new-world generation to reconstruction-grounded resimulation

LidarVerse brings generative and reconstruction-based LiDAR simulation into one conditional world model. The inference regime is determined by the available scene evidence, not by a separate architecture or prediction head.

Scene-level conditions guide new-world generation; an observed LiDAR prefix turns the same model into a point-cloud forecaster; and sparse observed sweeps form a persistent spatial cache that anchors generalizable resimulation without per-scene optimization. The distilled causal checkpoint runs at 10 FPS on a single accelerator, while the LiDAR tokenizer supports 32-, 64-, and 128-beam sensors.

System

One system, from compression to real-time interaction

01

Universal LiDAR tokenizer

A fully attention-based range-image tokenizer preserves sparse geometry and native temporal resolution in a shared latent space across scanning patterns.

02

Unified bidirectional teacher

Initialized from a pretrained video diffusion backbone and adapted to LiDAR latents, a flow-matching DiT learns one interface for the LiDAR prefix, HD-Map, spatial cache, and text.

03

Four-step causal student

Teacher forcing initializes a block-autoregressive student, and self-forcing distribution matching enables four-step generation of each four-frame block.

04

Temporal and spatial memory

A rolling nine-frame K/V cache retains recent dynamics, while a world-aligned persistent spatial cache preserves confirmed geometry beyond that temporal window.
